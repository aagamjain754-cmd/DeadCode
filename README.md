## DeadCode
-DeadCode is a horror-themed developer portfolio. Instead of the usual boring black and white resume site, I decided to make something that actually scares people. When you click the SKILLS section, there's a jumpscare waiting for you. It's meant to be memorable and fun, not annoying.

---------

## Concept
-Basically, you visit the portfolio, scroll through my work and projects, and when you get curious about the SKILLS section and click it - boom. You get a jumpscare. Just once though. After that it works normally. The whole thing has dark vibes with red accents, creepy music in the background, and smooth animations throughout.
